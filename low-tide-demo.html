How a session runs

  1. 1. Read the tide tableWe pick a two-hour window around the state of tide you need.
  2. 2. Set the rigTwo microphone pairs go out, one near the waterline and one back from it.
  3. 3. Wait out the windWe log gusts and boats, then keep rolling until the air settles.
  4. 4. Cut and masterYou get edited stems and untouched takes within ten days.
